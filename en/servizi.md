We deliver takeaway food from our kitchens to your door: pick your dishes in the [shop]({{ '/en/shop/' | relative_url }}), add them to the cart and order.

## Where we deliver
We deliver to the following postcodes:

- **00013**: Mentana, Tor Lupara, Fonte Nuova, Santa Lucia
- **00015**: Monterotondo and Monterotondo Scalo

## How to order
Choose the kitchen and dishes, complete your order from the cart and enter the delivery address. For questions or special requests, write to us from the contact page.
