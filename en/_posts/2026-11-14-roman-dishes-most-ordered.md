When people order food at home, the winners are few, familiar and easy to try. In Roman cuisine the podium is clear, according to the delivery platforms' rankings.

## What is ordered most
- **Supplì** (fried rice croquettes): first among Roman dishes in Deliveroo's ranking for the Birthday of Rome, and first outside Rome too.
- **Amatriciana, carbonara, Roman pinsa, gricia**: the dishes that follow supplì in the same ranking.
- **Artichoke**: the most ordered Roman dish nationwide, also according to Deliveroo.
- **East Rome**: in Tor Bella Monaca the podium is supplì, margherita pizza and diavola pizza (AGI, March 2026).

## Carbonara: loved, but hard to deliver
94% of Italians link carbonara to Rome (Sky TG24, Carbonara Day 2026). It also does well as street food: carbonara supplì, carbonara burger and mezze maniche alla carbonara are among the most ordered variants (Just Eat, 2023).

Careful: in delivery carbonara tends to set and lose its creaminess. It must be tested with a real delivery before going on the menu.

## Where we start
Supplì is the ideal starter: cheap, finger food and risk-free to try. Roman pasta dishes come right after.

*The rankings quoted come from delivery platforms and different years: use them as a guide, not as a sales guarantee.*
